## Upgrading a plan

An upgrade takes effect immediately. The customer is charged a prorated amount
for the remainder of the current billing period, calculated from the day the
upgrade is made.

## Downgrading a plan

A downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No refund is
issued for the unused portion of the higher plan, because the customer keeps
the higher plan's features until the period ends.

## Cancellation

A subscription can be cancelled at any time and remains active until the end of
the paid period. Cancelling does not trigger a refund. A customer who cancels
within 14 days of their very first payment is entitled to a full refund of that
first payment.

## Failed payments

A failed payment is retried on days 1, 3 and 7. The subscription is suspended
after the third failed retry. A suspended subscription is restored immediately
once a successful payment is taken; no data is deleted during suspension.

## Seat changes on Enterprise agreements

applies_to: enterprise

Seat counts on an Enterprise agreement are adjusted at the contract level, not
in the billing portal. Any seat change is confirmed in writing by the assigned
success manager before it takes effect.
