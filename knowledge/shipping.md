## Delivery estimates

Standard delivery arrives within 5 to 7 business days of dispatch. Express
delivery arrives within 2 business days. Delivery estimates begin from the
dispatch date shown on the order, not from the date the order was placed.

## Late deliveries

An order is considered late when it has not arrived 3 business days after the
latest estimated delivery date. A late order qualifies for a refund of the
shipping charge. The value of the goods is not automatically refunded for
lateness alone.

## Lost parcels

A parcel with no carrier tracking movement for 10 consecutive days is treated
as lost. A lost parcel is replaced at no cost to the customer, or refunded in
full if the item is out of stock. The customer is not asked to contact the
carrier themselves.

## Address changes

A delivery address can be changed only while the order status is "processing".
Once an order has dispatched the address cannot be changed and the parcel must
complete delivery or be returned to sender before a new order is placed.

## International shipping and customs

International orders may be held by customs. Duties and import taxes are the
responsibility of the recipient and are not refundable by us. A parcel held by
customs for more than 21 days is treated as lost.
