## Standard refund window

Customers on the Standard plan may request a full refund within 30 days of the
order date. Refunds are returned to the original payment method and settle
within 5 to 10 business days. Orders older than 30 days are not eligible for an
automatic refund and must be reviewed by a billing specialist.

## Enterprise refund window

applies_to: enterprise

Customers on the Enterprise plan may request a full refund within 90 days of
the order date. Enterprise refunds above 5000 cents always require approval
from the account's assigned success manager before the money moves, regardless
of how clear the request is.

## Refunds for damaged or defective goods

An order that arrives damaged or defective is refundable at any time within one
year of the order date. The customer does not need to return the item when the
refund amount is below 2000 cents. Photographic evidence is requested but is
not a condition of the refund.

## Partial refunds

A partial refund may be issued when only part of a multi-item order is
affected. The refunded amount must not exceed the price paid for the affected
items. Shipping charges are refunded only when the entire order is returned.

## Non-refundable items

Gift cards, digital download codes that have been redeemed, and clearance items
marked "final sale" are not refundable under any plan. A customer asking for a
refund on a final-sale item should be offered store credit instead.
