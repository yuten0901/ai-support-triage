## Password reset

A customer who cannot sign in should use the self-service password reset link
on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes. Support agents
must never set or communicate a password on the customer's behalf.

## Locked accounts

An account is locked automatically after 10 consecutive failed sign-in
attempts. The lock clears on its own after 30 minutes. Support may not unlock
an account early without verifying the customer's identity against the account
email and the last four digits of the payment method on file.

## Multi-factor authentication recovery

A customer who has lost their multi-factor device must complete identity
verification with a human agent. Multi-factor recovery is never handled
automatically, regardless of how confident the request appears, because an
automated recovery path is the exact path an account takeover would use.

## Email address changes

Changing the email address on an account requires confirmation from both the
old and the new address. If the customer no longer controls the old address the
request must be escalated to Trust and Safety.
